Projects

🔴 Smart Traffic Light System for Emergency Vehicles

A responsive traffic control solution that detects emergency vehicles and adjusts traffic lights dynamically, reducing response times and improving road safety.

🕒 Digital Clock – Verilog HDL

A 24-hour digital clock designed in Verilog using modular counters for seconds, minutes, and hours. It includes clock division logic, reset, and enable controls.

🪴 Talking Groot

A fun and interactive project where a plant (Groot!) talks based on its environmental sensor data — merging the world of plants and personalities!

🤟 American Sign Language Detection

A real-time system using a CNN model to recognize ASL gestures from camera input and convert them into text and speech — aiding communication for the hearing-impaired.

A synchronous FIFO buffer with configurable width and depth. Implements read/write pointers, full/empty flags, and safe data transfer.
